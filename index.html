Method. Frais de notaire are computed on the vendor price — buyer-borne agency fees are excluded from the DMTO base, which is the correct treatment when the fee is à la charge de l'acquéreur. Works are quoted HT, grossed up by contingency then soft costs, and TVA is applied at the rate selected. Debt is sized on total project cost; build-period interest assumes the average drawdown shown. Exit A prices the loi-Carrez area, not the habitable area. Plus-value abattements follow the statutory schedule (IR exempt at 22 years, prélèvements sociaux at 30); the surtaxe uses the simplified bracket table. Marchand de biens is taxed at IS 15 % to 42 500 € then 25 %. TVA sur marge is charged on the sale-minus-acquisition margin; the full-price option recovers input TVA on the works.

This is a modelling tool, not advice. It will not tell you whether a division defeats TVA sur marge, whether your works cross the immeuble neuf threshold, or whether a flip gets requalified as BIC. Those need a notaire and a fiscaliste. Rates and thresholds are hard-coded as of September 2026 — check them before you rely on a number.
